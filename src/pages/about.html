Our Story

Our Story

Covexall was created by a team of parents, scientists, and everyday problem-solvers who wanted better protection for the people they love most.

We were tired of products that washed off too quickly, irritated skin, or were harsh on kids. So we created a water-based, alcohol-free formula that forms a microscopic shield on your skin, keeping working for up to four hours.

Simple. Powerful. Worry-free. That's Covexall.

  • Born from a parent's promise to protect.
  • Developed with science. Designed for real life.
  • 🇺🇸Made in the USA with care and quality.
  • Trusted by families across the country.
