Your questions.
Clear answers.

Everything you need to know about Covexall hand protection.

How long does protection last?

Up to four hours of protection per application. Covexall forms a microscopic shield that bonds to your skin and keeps working after it dries. Reapply after washing hands or after four hours.

What does “kills 99.9% on contact” mean?

In independent laboratory testing, Covexall achieved a complete kill of 11 bacterial species, including MRSA, E. coli and C. difficile, within 60 seconds of exposure.

Is Covexall alcohol-free?

Yes. Covexall is a water-based formula with no alcohol, so it won't dry, crack or sting your skin, and it's safe around kids, classrooms and workplaces.

What is the active ingredient?

Benzalkonium Chloride (0.12–0.13%) combined with copper-infused magnesium hydroxide microplatelets, the technology that keeps Covexall working long after ordinary sanitizers have evaporated.

Is it safe for kids and sensitive skin?

Covexall is water-based, alcohol-free and non-irritating, and is formulated for everyday use by the whole family. As with any product, keep out of eyes and consult a doctor for specific skin conditions.

Where is Covexall made?

Covexall is manufactured in the United States in an FDA cGMP-compliant facility.
