Covexall Family Size Hand Sanitizer is our largest bottle for families, classrooms, workspaces, and shared environments.

Our unique formula kills 99.9% of germs on contact and keeps working for up to four hours, without harsh alcohol or sticky residue.

  • Lab Tested
  • Made in the USA
  • Cruelty Free
  • FDA cGMP Facility

Active ingredient: Benzalkonium Chloride 0.12–0.13%.

Technology: copper-infused magnesium hydroxide microplatelets that bond to skin and keep the formula working after it dries.

Water-based. Alcohol-free. Fragrance-free. Dye-free.

  1. Pump a small amount into your palm.
  2. Rub hands together, covering fingers and wrists.
  3. Let dry for a few seconds. No rinsing needed.
  4. Reapply after washing hands or after four hours.
Is the pump bottle refillable?

Yes. The 500ml bottle can be refilled from Covexall refill sizes.

Is it safe around babies and toddlers?

Covexall is alcohol-free and non-irritating. Keep out of eyes and mouth, as with any hand product.

Does it leave residue?

No. It dries fast with no sticky feel or strong smell.
